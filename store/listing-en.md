## Detailed description

```
Onliner Price Tracker watches product prices on catalog.onliner.by and keeps a private history on your device.

What you get
• Watch / unwatch from any catalog product page (user-initiated only)
• Background price checks about every 3 hours (plus Check now in the popup)
• Local price history with a small chart in the extension popup
• Chrome notification when the latest minimum price is lower than before

How it works
1. Open a product on catalog.onliner.by
2. Click “Watch price” (bottom-right)
3. Open the extension popup to review watches and charts
4. Allow notifications when Chrome asks — you’ll be alerted on price drops

Privacy
• Watches and history stay in chrome.storage.local on your computer
• Network requests go only to catalog.onliner.by and catalog.api.onliner.by for products you watch
• No accounts, analytics, ads, or sale of personal data

Notes
• Only products you explicitly watch are checked (no catalog crawl)
• Requests are spaced (~1.5s between products) to keep load modest
• Prices use the public product API (prices.price_min), with page fallback when needed
```
